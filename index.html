Hour Time

A simple, private way to log your field and outreach work.

Track your hours, remember the people you visit, and never miss a follow-up, whether you go door to door, staff a booth, write letters, or make calls.

Free for up to 15 people · Full Version is a one-time purchase, no subscription

Scan with your iPhone camera

What it does

Track hours

Start a timer when you head out, or add time by hand later. Credit hours are kept separately.

Stay on pace

See how you're doing against your monthly goal at a glance, and review every entry by day.

Remember people

Keep notes, addresses, phone numbers, and a history of every visit.

Never miss a follow-up

Set reminders for return visits and get notified on time.

See it on a map

View everyone nearby and get directions in one tap.

Yours alone

No account or sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

Pricing

Hour Time is free to download and use for up to 15 people, with every feature included. A one-time Full Version purchase removes the limit so you can add as many people as you like. There is no subscription.
