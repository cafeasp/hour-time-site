Hour Time

Support & contact

Hour Time is a personal field-visit and outreach tracking app for iPhone. It logs your time, keeps track of the people you visit, sets reminders, and shows everything on a map — with your data stored privately in your own iCloud account.

Need help? Email vpacheco99@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version so I can help faster.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own private iCloud account. There is no account to create and no developer-operated server — I cannot see your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My data isn't syncing between my devices

Hour Time syncs through iCloud, so both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on. Check Settings → [your name] → iCloud on each device, then make sure Hour Time is enabled. Syncing can take a moment on a slow or metered connection.

I paid to unlock the full version but it still looks locked

Open the app's unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them, so make sure you are signed in to that same account on the device.

What does the full version unlock?

The free version lets you add up to 15 people. The one-time in-app purchase removes that limit. Everything else — time logging, reminders, reports, and the map — is included for free, and unlocking never hides or deletes records you already created.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records inside the app, or remove everything by deleting the app and clearing its iCloud data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email vpacheco99@gmail.com with what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
